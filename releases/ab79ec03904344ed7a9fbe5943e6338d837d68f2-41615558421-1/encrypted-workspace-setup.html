Use your own encrypted CV workspace

This connection uses your own Google Sheet, Form and processing script. The Sheet is publicly readable, but CV text, filenames, job details and redaction mappings are encrypted in your browser before upload. Public viewers cannot authorize changes without a paired browser’s signing key.

First setup

  1. Use desktop Chrome or Brave and a personal Gmail account. Copy the encrypted template linked in the app’s workspace settings. If the app says the template is not published, the app owner must complete the preparation below first.
  2. Open your copied Sheet. Choose Encrypted workspace → Set up or connect. If the menu is missing, reload the Sheet. Review Google’s permissions under the account that owns this copy.
  3. Read the public-storage disclosure and initialize your copy. This creates your own Form and a processing trigger. Do not put plaintext CVs, names or other private information in worksheet cells.
  4. Enter the CV app’s full address into the Google connection panel. Check it carefully: that website will receive access to your documents. Choose Open and pair this browser.
  5. In the app, confirm the displayed Sheet and owner. Choose a strong vault passphrase of at least 12 characters, or enter the existing passphrase for this workspace. There is no reset or recovery service.
  6. Run Pair and verify connection. The app saves, reads, updates and deletes an encrypted dummy document. Your browser remembers access only after these checks succeed. Keep the Google panel open until it finishes; then you may close the Sheet.
  7. Import your CV explicitly. Your new workspace starts empty; existing personal browser documents are not transferred.

Returning or using another computer

Returning visits use keys remembered in that browser profile, with no repeated passphrase prompt. On a new profile or after clearing browser data, reopen the same Sheet and pair again using your vault passphrase. Do not create another copy unless you want a new, empty workspace.

The personal Google connection extension belongs to Andrea’s API flow. Encrypted workspaces do not need it. Automatic tailoring still uses your own ChatGPT session and the separate ChatGPT Browser Bridge extension; manual prompts and local PDF compilation remain available.

Privacy and limits

This protects public storage, not your device: browser access keys and local working copies remain on the device. Anyone controlling that browser profile, its extensions or the app’s code may read your documents. Lost-device revocation, recovery and in-place passphrase changes are not provided.

Public visitors can see encrypted record IDs, sizes, activity and command history. They can submit junk to the Form and consume your Google trigger quota. Deleting a file removes it from the active library; encrypted history and copies held by others may remain.

Cloud saves can take time. Staging remains on by default. Keep the tab open during a save; only a verified acknowledgement marks it synced. If interrupted, preserve your browser data and retry the staged save. Conflicts require review.

Stopping use

Workspace settings can disconnect this browser without removing its local working copies. You own the Google resources and can delete them. The spreadsheet, linked Google Form and installed Apps Script trigger are separate resources: inspect and remove each if retiring the workspace. This cannot retract downloaded ciphertext or plaintext.

App owner: prepare the template once

The encrypted template is separate from the legacy Forms experiment and Andrea’s private Sheet. Existing templates do not acquire new code automatically.

  1. Download the encrypted connector source ZIP from this build. Its text files contain no credentials or user data.
  2. Create a blank Sheet under a test Gmail account other than Andrea’s. Open Extensions → Apps Script. Install the ZIP’s Menu, Vault and Crypto text as script files, VaultPanel as HTML, and appsscript as the manifest. Remove the default empty script if needed. Keep the bundled license files with the distributed source.
  3. Save, reload the Sheet and run the first-setup steps above with dummy data. Test a fresh owner copy, a public viewer, two browser profiles and the maximum-size dummy master before using real CVs. Local automated checks do not verify Google authorization, popup pairing or real trigger delivery.
  4. Keep an uninitialized, empty copy as the shareable template. Share it for copying and set VITE_ENCRYPTED_TEMPLATE_URL to its copy URL in the app build. Confirm copies create independent resources. Do not publish a template containing paired public keys, a wrapped workspace key, CV data or initialized command history.

Connector updates require separate Google-side installation and validation. If a future version is incompatible, retain local work and export it; do not reset or replace a populated workspace as an automatic repair.
